# Release Planning

# Release planning

Release planning consists of two 2-hour meetings to let the team and community discuss what they plan to deliver in the upcoming release.

Brigades and project teams should review, scrub, and prioritize their backlogs prior to the release planning meeting in order to represent what they intend to work on and deliver in the upcoming release.

The release planning meetings take place at 8-10 am and 4-6 pm PST/PDT.  A template slideset is emailed out (a few days prior to the planning date) to the ONOS dev mailing list/Google group for the brigades and project teams to fill in the details of their planned deliveries.

# Release demo

Release demo may consist of two 1-hour meetings to let the team and community show what they delivered in the release.  The meetings take place at 8 am and 5 pm PST/PDT.  Meetings in corresponding time zones are not held if no demo is available to be shown.
