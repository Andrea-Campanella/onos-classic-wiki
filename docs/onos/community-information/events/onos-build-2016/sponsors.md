# Sponsors

Support of our sponsors is instrumental in enhancing the quality of the event and will also help cover the costs for travel and accommodation of some of our talented student participants who otherwise may not be able to participate in the conference.

Benefits for sponsors include: 

* A way to support the ONOS community and accelerate the adoption of a more community-led approach to development
* A unique opportunity for your company’s brand and message to be exposed to the largest gathering of ONOS developers, Community at a single event.
* Extensive press coverage and media outreach
* A seat on the "Industry Panel" on Day 2 of the event

### Sponsorship Types

* PLATINUM $10,000 USD

+ Complimentary passes for the ONOS Build 2016 conference
+ Logo on all conference programs
+ Logo on conference web site, with link to your company website
+ Full-page company-supplied description in the ONOS Build 2016 final program
+ Explicit naming of your company at the welcome dinner/closing dinner to highlight the conference patronage
+ Company-supplied banner prominently displayed during the entire Conference

* GOLD $5,000 USD

+ Complimentary passes to ONOS Build 2016 conference
+ Logo on all conference programs
+ Logo on conference web site, with link to your company website
+ Half-page company-supplied description in the ONOS Build 2016 final program

* SILVER $2,500 USD

+ Complimentary passes to ONOS Build 2016 conference
+ Logo on all conference programs
+ Logo on conference web site, with link to your company website
+ Quarter-page company-supplied description in the ONOS Build 2016 final program
