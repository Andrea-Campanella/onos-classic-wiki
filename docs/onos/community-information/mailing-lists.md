# Mailing Lists

[Link](https://wiki.opennetworking.org/x/AoBEDw)ONOS uses Google Groups for its mailing lists. Unless otherwise noted, they are all public - anyone can join, follow and post.

To join ONOS groups, click on the links below. If you are in a company or region that does not allow you to join by clicking on these links you can always send an email to <group>+subscribe@onosproject.org

For example, to join onos-dev, send an email to:  
[onos-dev+subscribe@onosproject.org](mailto:onos-dev%2Bsubscribe@onosproject.org)

and then reply to the subscription confirmation email.

When adding a new list add it to the following table with a link to the google group to make it easy to join or visit the group.

Make sure to **include the list**when replying to posts via email. This ensures that everyone is able to see and contribute to the discussion.
